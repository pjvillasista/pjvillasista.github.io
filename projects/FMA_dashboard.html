FMA Marketing Analytics Dashboard

Python Excel Tableau

Instagram engagement analysis for the Financial Management Association at CSULB. Identified which content drove engagement, when to post, and what the data showed sponsors — contributing to $8,000 in funding, 50% event attendance lift, and 25% membership growth.

The Challenge

FMA's Instagram posts went out without any data-driven strategy — no analysis of which post types drove follows vs. saves vs. comments, no insight into optimal posting windows, and no structured evidence to bring to sponsor conversations. Leadership needed both an internal tool for the social media team and a polished dashboard to show sponsors that the org had reach worth investing in.

The Approach

1. Data Collection: Exported Instagram Insights data covering post-level engagement metrics — likes, comments, saves, reach, impressions, and profile visits per post — across several months of activity.

2. Python Analysis: Used Python and Pandas to clean the export, parse post metadata (type, caption, hashtags, day/time), and compute engagement rate per post. Analyzed distributions by content type (photo vs. reel vs. carousel) and by day-of-week and hour-of-day posting windows.

3. Identifying Patterns: Carousel posts drove 2–3x the save rate of single-image posts. Tuesday and Thursday evening posts consistently outperformed Monday morning posts on reach. Hashtag clusters tied to finance recruiting performed better than generic #CSULB tags.

4. Tableau KPI Dashboard: Built an executive-facing dashboard showing follower growth over time, engagement rate trends, top-performing posts, and content-type breakdown — designed to be readable by sponsors with no analytics background.
